GOVERN YOUR WAY

START FREE. SHAPE THE FUTURE.
• Free prologue: 2000–2004.
• Main campaign: 2005–2026, with historical challenges and your own political response. The US entries for 2025–2026 are fictional policy scenarios.

This is an independent entertainment game inspired by history. Responses, outcomes and election mechanics belong to a simplified alternate-history simulation. Future scenarios are fictional, not predictions. The game is not affiliated with political parties and does not endorse a candidate or party.

## App-Prüfung

No account or sign-in is required. Version 1.7 adds a country selection on launch. Choose United States for the English US campaign or Germany for the existing German campaign. Each country has separate saved progress; purchased content rights are shared.

Review flow: select United States, choose a party and play the free prologue (2000–2004). The main campaign (2005–2026) is unlocked by regiere_deutschland_one_time and includes founding a party, chronicle and statistics. regiere_deutschland_one_time_two unlocks the fictional Future 2050 expansion (2026–2050), requiring the main campaign. regiere_deutschland_complett_packet_one unlocks both. These are the existing non-consumable products, not new US-specific purchases.

All purchases are permanent one-time purchases, with no subscription or trial. Purchases and Restore Purchases use Apple's StoreKit through RevenueCat and require internet. No external checkout is used. Open Campaigns & bundles on the home screen for offers or Restore purchases. The app uses English purchase screens for the US campaign and retains German screens for Germany.

A purchase at the end of the prologue or main campaign continues the same save. The future expansion includes 200 scenario decisions and 75 additional events. Future events and US entries for 2025–2026 are explicitly fictional. A world war is avoidable. The US campaign follows successive party administrations, with simplified presidential elections and congressional support; it does not claim that one president may serve for 50 years.
