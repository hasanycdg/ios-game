Your decisions. America's future.

Lead the United States through elections, crises and reform, or choose Germany for its original German-language campaign. Pick your country when you launch the app, build your party's support and see how your choices shape the decades ahead.

TWO COUNTRIES. TWO POLITICAL WORLDS.
• United States: American parties, presidential elections, Congress, cabinet appointments and diplomacy.
• Germany: the existing campaign with German parties, coalition negotiations and the chancellery, presented in German.
• Country-specific events and decisions.
• Separate saves and progress, so you can switch countries without losing your other campaign.

GOVERN YOUR WAY
• Set your course on the economy, public spending, security, energy and foreign policy.
• Campaign for office, negotiate political support and appoint your cabinet.
• Watch public approval, government finances and the reactions of different groups.
• Face consequences that can emerge years after a decision.
• Found your own party and explore your chronicle and statistics with the main campaign.

START FREE. SHAPE THE FUTURE.
• Free prologue: 2000–2004.
• Main campaign: 2005–2026, with historical challenges and your own political response. The US entries for 2025–2026 are fictional policy scenarios.
• Future 2050: a fictional expansion spanning 2026–2050, with 200 scenario decisions and 75 additional events in a complete playthrough.

Prevent escalation, guide reconstruction and help shape a new world order. A world war can be avoided. Earlier choices change later consequences and your final legacy. The expansion continues your existing save.

Play turn by turn at your own pace, without a game account. The US campaign follows successive administrations of a party across the decades.

ABOUT THE SIMULATION
This is an independent entertainment game inspired by history. Responses, outcomes and election mechanics belong to a simplified alternate-history simulation. Future scenarios are fictional, not predictions. The game is not affiliated with political parties and does not endorse a candidate or party.
